Own your network.

Grade every relationship.

Legend isn't a social network — there's no feed, no followers, no algorithm. It's a personal tool that tells you, honestly, how strong and how fresh each relationship is, and helps you act on it. Your contacts are yours: import and export freely, and keep everything on your device.

The Grade

Relationships, ranked like loot

Every contact earns a score from the interactions you log — a visit is worth more than a text, and every touch fades over time, so relationships you neglect naturally cool off. That score maps to a video-game rarity tier you can read at a glance.

Common0–19
Uncommon20–39
Rare40–59
Epic60–79
Legendary80–100

What Legend Does

Built around five ideas

📋

Contacts, but better

An alphabetized, instantly searchable list that beats your phone's Contacts app — with a Recent view and one-tap call, text, or email.

🧩

The Premise

The structured "why you know each other" — an event, topic, expertise, hobby, or place — plus where you met, captured when you add them.

⭐

The Grade

A freshness + strength score with rarity colors, tunable to your own rules: weight each interaction, set the decay, choose your palette.

🎯

Circles of Influence

Pick a premise — a tag, a topic, an event — and Legend surfaces everyone who matches, ready to reach as a group.

💬

Outreach that respects the OS

Bulk texts one-by-one through your Messages app, BCC email blasts, or a stalest-first call list — every send logs the touch automatically.

💾

You own your data

One-tap CSV export of everything, import from your phone or any spreadsheet with column mapping, and a QR code to share your own card.
